CLEAR ANSWERS

Good to know.

Useful details before signing in, buying, downloading or chatting.

Does website sign-in also sign me into Minecraft?

No. The VoidCraft website account manages your web profile. It is not Microsoft/Xbox/Minecraft authentication and does not verify game ownership.

Can I buy cosmetics right now?

The Cosmetic Market may show preview listings. A cosmetic can only be purchased after a real price, released package and secure payment configuration are active. Website equip does not by itself render a cosmetic in Minecraft.

Is Social Hub using the same account?

Not yet. Social Hub is an independently authenticated staging beta. Use a unique password there. The same VoidCraft visual branding does not mean the accounts are connected.
