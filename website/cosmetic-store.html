Purchase first, then equip

Cosmetic cards show Equip only after the store confirms payment and records ownership on your account. The current Equip control applies the purchased cosmetic to this website profile; it does not yet render on a Minecraft player model or make the cosmetic visible to multiplayer players. In-game visibility requires a compatible Minecraft client renderer and, for multiplayer, compatible server support.
